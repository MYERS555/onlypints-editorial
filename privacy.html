Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

OnlyPints Editorial is an internal analytics and editorial planning tool used by the owners of the Only Pints social media brand.

Information we access

When an authorised TikTok account is connected, the tool may access information permitted by the account owner, including basic profile information, account statistics, and metadata and engagement statistics for public TikTok videos.

How information is used

Information is used only to analyse content performance, maintain historical analytics, and support editorial and content-planning decisions for Only Pints.

Storage

Authorised TikTok data and access credentials are stored locally on the operator's computer. The tool is not designed to sell personal data or make TikTok account data available to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Sharing

Data is not sold. Data may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to authenticate the account and access the TikTok APIs requested by the operator.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored analytics may be retained for historical performance analysis until the operator deletes them. TikTok authorisation can be revoked through TikTok account settings, after which the tool will no longer be able to obtain new authorised data unless access is granted again.
